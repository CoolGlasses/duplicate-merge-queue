# Terms of Service — Duplicate Merge Queue

Last updated: 2026-11-14

## The service
Duplicate Merge Queue scans a connected HubSpot portal for duplicate
contacts and companies and presents proposed merges to a human
operator. No merge is executed without explicit approval. On approval,
the service snapshots both records and then calls HubSpot's merge API.

## Your responsibilities
- You are responsible for reviewing proposals before approving them.
  A merge in HubSpot cannot be automatically reversed; the pre-merge
  snapshot the service keeps is the recovery record, and restoring a
  merged record is a manual process performed from that snapshot.
- You must have the right to connect the portal and to merge records
  in it.

## Plans and billing
The free tier provides scanning and a duplicate count. The paid portal
plan ($29/month per connected portal) enables the review queue and
merges. Subscriptions are billed through Stripe and renew monthly
until cancelled; cancelling returns the portal to the free tier.
