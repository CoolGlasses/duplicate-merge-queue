# Privacy Policy — Duplicate Merge Queue

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Duplicate Merge Queue connects to your HubSpot portal with your
authorization to find duplicate contacts and companies and to execute
merges that a human operator explicitly approves.

## What we access
- Contact and company records in the connected portal (names, email
  addresses, phone numbers, company names and domains, and standard
  CRM fields) — read to detect duplicates, and written only to execute
  an approved merge.
- Before any approved merge, we store a snapshot of both records in
  the pair. Snapshots exist so a merge can be audited and, if needed,
  manually undone from the snapshot data.

## What we never do
- We never merge, edit, or delete a record without an explicit human
  approval in the review queue.
- We never sell or share your CRM data, and we never use it to train
  models.
- We do not access deals or any object other than contacts and
  companies.

## Storage and security
Data is stored on the service's host in an access-controlled store.
HubSpot OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. Snapshots and the merge
audit log are retained while your portal is connected so merges stay
auditable; disconnect the app and contact us to have stored data for
your portal deleted.

## Payments
Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store
your card details.
